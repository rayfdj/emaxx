** Recipes that actually worked
*** Weeknight dal
Red lentils, one cup, rinsed until the water runs clear.  Onion,
cumin seeds bloomed in ghee, garlic, ginger, one chopped tomato,
turmeric, salt.  Simmer twenty minutes, finish with a second tadka
of ghee, mustard seeds and dried chili poured over the top at the
table because the sizzle is the whole point.
*** Saturday focaccia
500g flour, 400g water, 10g salt, 4g yeast.  Mix, four folds over
two hours, overnight in the fridge.  Dimple with more olive oil
than seems reasonable, then a little more.  230C for 25 minutes.
The rosemary goes on before the bake, the flaky salt after.

* Notes
** On keeping this file
The rule that makes this work: everything lands in the journal
first, and only gets promoted to a project or a note when it has
survived two weeks of being looked at.  A todo that cannot survive
two weeks of being looked at was a mood, not a task, and moods do
not need scheduling, they need coffee or a walk or admitting that
the thing is not actually important to you no matter how important
it feels at eleven at night after reading someone else's blog.
** Terminal emulators and fonts, an opinion nobody asked for
After trying six of them the conclusion is that they are all fine
and the differences that matter are (a) whether the font renders
the box-drawing characters at exactly cell height and (b) input
latency you can only barely perceive but your hands somehow know
about anyway.  Everything else is a theme picker with a venture
round.  The font situation is simpler: you use the one you used
last year, because your eyes have learned it, and switching costs
two weeks of mild irritation to save zero measurable minutes.
** Half-formed idea: a garden log as a time series
Soil temperature, watering minutes, harvest weight per bed.  A
season of data and then actual decisions next spring instead of
vibes.  The sensors are cheap; the discipline is not.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/show-major-modes ()
  "List every live buffer with its major mode."
  (interactive)
  (with-current-buffer (get-buffer-create "*modes*")
    (erase-buffer)
    (dolist (b (buffer-list))
      (insert (format "%-30s %s\n"
                      (buffer-name b)
                      (buffer-local-value 'major-mode b))))
    (display-buffer (current-buffer))))
